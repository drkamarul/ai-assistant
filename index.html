Before you come

Finish these at least one week before the workshop. The setup guide shows every step (about 60–90 minutes).

  • R and Positron installed on your laptop (Positron includes Quarto). We do not use RStudio or Posit Cloud.
  • A folder called workshop in your Documents folder — your project for the whole day.
  • The check script printed All set! in Positron.
  • Google Antigravity 2.0 (the Antigravity App, not the Antigravity IDE) installed and signed in.
  • Antigravity can find R (Rscript --version works).
  • Laptop charger packed.

Use a personal Gmail for Antigravity. Some organisations' Google accounts (university or workplace) block Google Antigravity.

Claude subscribers (Pro or higher): you are encouraged to install Claude Desktop too. Not needed for the exercises.

Stuck? Email drki.musa@gmail.com with a screenshot and your laptop type.
